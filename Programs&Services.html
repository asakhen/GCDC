Our Core Programs & Services

GCDC is dedicated to empowering local communities through targeted social programs, educational workshops, and nutritional support.

Key Initiatives

1. Youth Development & Workshops

We provide after-school tutoring, mentorship, leadership training, and practical skill-building workshops for youth in the community.

2. Community Feeding Schemes

Our nutritional assistance program serves daily meals to vulnerable families, children, and elderly residents to tackle food insecurity.

3. Skills Development & Enterprise

Offering vocational training and entrepreneurial guidance to help adults gain steady employment or launch micro-enterprises.
