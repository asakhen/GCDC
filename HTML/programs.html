Our Programs & Services

Empowering the youth and residents of Gugulethu through accessible skills development, healthcare initiatives, and social support services.

Key Initiatives & Eligibility

Education & Digital Skills

Offering practical computer training, coding basics, and digital literacy workshops to equip local youth for employment and further education.

Target Audience: Local Youth (Ages 16-35)

Eligibility: Open to all Gugulethu residents; prior registration required.

Youth Leadership & Mentorship

Structured career guidance, life skills coaching, and peer mentorship to build confidence and community leadership among young people.

Target Audience: High School Students & School Leavers

Eligibility: Walk-in access during scheduled days.

Community Feeding Schemes

Providing daily nutritious meals and food parcel distribution for vulnerable families and school children across Gugulethu.

Target Audience: Vulnerable Households & Children

Eligibility: Community referral or direct registration at GCDC office.
